## Scope and variants

Android `<FrameLayout>` compiles to `bhoo::android_xml::Kind::Frame` and stacks children with start, center, or end gravity on each axis. `tools/import-android-layout.py` also lowers `<androidx.coordinatorlayout.widget.CoordinatorLayout>` / `<CoordinatorLayout>` (plus `--merge-parent CoordinatorLayout`), `<CollapsingToolbarLayout>`, `<DrawerLayout>`, `<SwipeRefreshLayout>` (`androidx.swiperefreshlayout.widget.SwipeRefreshLayout` and legacy `android.support.v4.widget.SwipeRefreshLayout`), `<com.google.android.material.search.SearchView>`, `<MaskableFrameLayout>`, and `<FragmentContainerView>` to `Kind::Frame`, while `Kind::MediaController`, `Kind::MaterialCard`, `Kind::ViewAnimator`, `Kind::ViewSwitcher`, `Kind::TextSwitcher`, `Kind::ImageSwitcher`, and `Kind::ViewFlipper` share `FrameLayout`'s active-`TextMetrics` wrap-width measurement path.

## State and interaction contract

Children retain their own state and can overlap. `measureAllChildren` includes gone children in measurement while keeping them hidden. Margins and fixed, match-parent and approximate wrap-content sizing affect placement. Start and end gravity follow the caller-selected layout direction; left and right remain absolute. Relative start/end margins resolve with direction and override physical margins. TextView children with wrap-content height are measured using their available width. Fixed-size children retain their requested size even when larger than the parent. `android:clipToPadding` controls clipping to the padded area, and `android:clipChildren` controls clipping each child to its own bounds; ancestor clipping still applies. Background shape intrinsic size contributes to wrap-content minimum dimensions; shape padding supplies default view padding, with XML view padding overriding it. Solid-color and simple solid shape foregrounds paint above children, including rounded rectangles and solid rectangular and oval strokes. Shapes without a solid fill remain transparent. Oval foregrounds and backgrounds scale to the view bounds. Foreground intrinsic size contributes to FrameLayout wrap-content minimums; fill-gravity foreground padding also affects child layout. Foreground gravity can position sized shapes, and foregroundInsidePadding controls its placement area. Full Android measurement and other drawable foregrounds remain unsupported.

### `CoordinatorLayout` scroll-below-AppBar and anchor placement (Wave 4A & 4B)

When `tools/import-android-layout.py` compiles a `CoordinatorLayout` (`<androidx.coordinatorlayout.widget.CoordinatorLayout>`, `<CoordinatorLayout>`, or `--merge-parent CoordinatorLayout`), direct children emit two `Kind::Frame` coordination rules (non-`CoordinatorLayout` `FrameLayout` parents do not emit these rules):

- **`RULE_COORDINATOR_SCROLL_BEHAVIOR = 226` (`anchor: ""`):** emitted when a direct child specifies `app:layout_behavior` referencing `appbar_scrolling_view_behavior` or `ScrollingViewBehavior` (either literally or through a resolved `@string/...` resource; other custom `Behavior` class names are accepted without emitting rule `226`).
  - At runtime (`compute_coordinator_rects` pass 1 in `codes/libs/ui/libbhoo/src/systems/android/xml/mod.rs`), each visible scrolling child finds the first preceding visible non-bottom `Kind::Linear` or `Kind::Toolbar` sibling (not itself carrying rule `226` or `227`, matching an `AppBarLayout` or direct `Toolbar`), shifts its layout area down by that appbar's bottom edge plus bottom margin (`ab_rect.y + ab_rect.height + ab_margin[3] - inner.y`), and reduces its available height accordingly. When the appbar is `Visibility::Gone`, the scrolling child shifts back to `y = inner.y`. Vertical `wrap_content` measurement (`layout_intrinsic`) also sums the appbar height with the scrolling child height.
- **`RULE_COORDINATOR_ANCHOR = 227` (`anchor: "<target_id>:<grav1>|<grav2>"`):** emitted when a direct child specifies a non-null `app:layout_anchor` (`@id/...` or `@+id/...`, rejecting invalid IDs) and optional `app:layout_anchorGravity` (defaulting to `center`, deduplicating tokens, and validating against `top`, `bottom`, `start`, `end`, `left`, `right`, `center`, `center_horizontal`, `center_vertical`, rejecting unknown gravity values).
  - At runtime (`compute_coordinator_rects` pass 2 + `resolve_coordinator_anchor_rect`), after all non-anchored siblings are laid out, each anchored child is measured and centered on the target sibling's resolved edge/corner anchor point (`start`/`end` mirror in RTL; `left`/`right` stay physical), then clamped within `inner` respecting its resolved margins (`margin_relative` mirrors in RTL). If the target sibling is missing or `Gone`, placement falls back to standard frame gravity.

### Z-order sibling pointer occlusion & clickable container child-first dispatch

- **Z-order overlapping sibling occlusion (`Frame::without_pointer_input` in [`composition.rs`](../../src/systems/material/mod.rs) + `blocks_pointer_for_lower_siblings_in` in [`android_xml.rs`](../../src/systems/android/xml/mod.rs)):** When `!f.any_scroll_dragging()`, any visible higher-z sibling in `Kind::Frame` / `CoordinatorLayout` whose `blocks_pointer_for_lower_siblings_in()` is `true` (clickable views, interactive controls, `BottomSheet`, `SideSheet`, `Snackbar`, `FloatingToolbar`, `AlertDialog`, `NavBar`, `SearchBar`, and open/animating `RULE_SEARCH_VIEW_EXT = 302` overlays — explicitly returning `false` when a `SearchView` overlay is closed so it never occludes lower siblings) and whose clipped bounds contain the pointer occludes lower siblings via `Frame::without_pointer_input` — blocking only pointer/wheel input while preserving keyboard focus and key events. Floating FABs and overlays no longer leak hover, press, ripple, or clicks to overlaid rows below.
- **Child-first pointer dispatch inside clickable `ViewGroup` containers:** Clickable containers with children (`defer_container_target`) evaluate children first and suppress pointer input on the container's own target when a child claims the pointer (`child_owns_pointer`), so nested clickable controls win hover/press/ripple/click without activating or rippling the parent container while edge-to-edge container padding outside children still clicks the parent.

## Motion and animation

Layout changes are applied directly ([Static motion profile](../motion.md#motion-profiles)); any child control supplies its own interaction motion.

**Reduced motion:** Layout remains directly positioned.

## Acceptance criteria

- Import combined horizontal and vertical layout gravity.
- Verify center and bottom/end positions with margins and start/end placement in both directions.
- Verify `CoordinatorLayout` scroll-below-AppBar offsetting (`RULE_COORDINATOR_SCROLL_BEHAVIOR = 226`, including `Visibility::Gone` appbars) and anchored sibling positioning (`RULE_COORDINATOR_ANCHOR = 227`) in both LTR and RTL.
- Compare sizing, CoordinatorLayout placement, and RTL behavior with Android on a device.

## Nested wrap-content width

A wrap-content FrameLayout measures the largest supported child width using the active text metrics, current child state, margins and padding; gone children are included only with `measureAllChildren`. Frame background and foreground minimums still apply. A focused example changes a nested button label and checks that a following sibling moves in LTR and RTL.

### FrameLayout subclass wrap-width fix (Wave 1B)

- **Cause and fix:** Horizontal `wrap_content` measurement in `Node::layout_intrinsic` (`codes/libs/ui/libbhoo/src/systems/android/xml/mod.rs`) previously applied active `TextMetrics` only to `Kind::Frame`, leaving FrameLayout subclasses on the fallback `intrinsic` path with default text metrics. `layout_intrinsic` now handles `Kind::Frame` together with all FrameLayout-derived containers (`Kind::MediaController`, `Kind::MaterialCard`, `Kind::ViewAnimator`, `Kind::ViewSwitcher`, `Kind::TextSwitcher`, `Kind::ImageSwitcher`, and `Kind::ViewFlipper`), honoring `measure_all_children`, child margins, resolved padding, `min_width`, `background_min_size`, and `foreground_size`.
