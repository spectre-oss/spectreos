# Android XML ImageButton

Status: Initial XML support for image-backed buttons (`Kind::Icon`), bundled PNG/9-patch/vector resources, Wave 11B default `centerInside` non-upscaling, Wave 14A & Wave 17A/17B single- and multi-segment `.9.png` slicing (`RULE_IMAGE_NINE_PATCH = 252`), stateful `src` selectors (`RULE_STATEFUL_IMAGE_SRC = 253`), and RTL `autoMirrored` (`RULE_AUTO_MIRRORED = 255`), Wave 18A/18B timed `<animation-list>` frame playback (`RULE_ANIMATION_LIST = 285`), Wave 19A/19B dynamic wrapper drawables (`RULE_DRAWABLE_DYNAMIC = 286` for `level-list`, `clip`, `rotate`, `scale`, and `transition`), Wave 20A/20B `<animated-selector>` transition frame playback (`RULE_ANIMATED_SELECTOR = 293`), Wave 24A/24B sampled `<animated-vector>` keyframe playback (`RULE_ANIMATED_VECTOR = 309`), plus borderless circular default state-layer ripple (`width.min(height) * 0.5`); Android theme raised button chrome, SVG `pathData` morphing, and device parity remain open.

## Scope and variants

Android `<ImageButton>` and `<androidx.appcompat.widget.AppCompatImageButton>` with a host-supplied or bundled PNG/rasterized `<vector>` drawable image (or built-in `MaterialIcon`), scaling, tinting, and enabled or disabled state.

## State and interaction contract

An enabled button reports activation by view ID. The importer can bundle supported PNG (including `.9.png`) and `<vector>` drawables or use the existing built-in icon mapping. Hosts can replace the drawable by view ID via `LayoutState.image_sources`, as for `ImageView`.

- **Default `centerInside` non-upscaling (Wave 11B):** In `codes/libs/ui/libbhoo/src/systems/android/xml/mod.rs`, when `Kind::Icon` paints a decoded/rasterized image with default `image_scale == 0` (`fitCenter`), `!image_adjust_bounds`, and no explicit padding (`padding_explicit == [false; 4]` and `padding_relative == [None, None]`), it selects `effective_scale = 4` (`centerInside`). Small icons (such as `24dp` vector/PNG icons, `36px` at `density = 1.5`) stay crisp and centered at their density-scaled intrinsic size inside `44dp`/`48dp` touch targets without upscaling, while oversized drawables still scale down cleanly to fit bounds. Explicit `android:scaleType` (e.g. `fitXY`), explicit padding, `android:adjustViewBounds="true"`, and `ImageView` (`Kind::Image`) scaling remain unchanged.
- **Single- and multi-segment 9-patch slicing, stateful `src` selectors & RTL `autoMirrored` (Wave 14A & Wave 17A/17B):** Supports `RULE_IMAGE_NINE_PATCH = 252` (single- and multi-segment UV stretch slicing via `decode_nine_patch_bands` and `nine_patch_axis_segments`, keeping fixed borders/dividers density-scaled while distributing remaining pixels proportionally across all stretch bands), `RULE_STATEFUL_IMAGE_SRC = 253` (live 7-bit state-mask `<selector>` switching including pressed/selected multi-band 9-patch items), and `RULE_AUTO_MIRRORED = 255` (horizontal `PushTransform` flip in RTL only).
- **Timed `<animation-list>` frame playback (Wave 18A & Wave 18B):** Supports `RULE_ANIMATION_LIST = 285` (`"<oneshot:0|1>|<img_key_0>:<dur_ms_0>,..."`) across `android:src` / `app:srcCompat` (bundling PNG, `.9.png`, `<vector>`, and inline `<shape>` frames in `tools/import-android-layout.py` with frame-0 fallback and cycle rejection). At runtime in `codes/libs/ui/libbhoo/src/systems/android/xml/mod.rs`, `Kind::Icon` selects frames from `f.time()`, schedules `f.wake_at(...)` wakeups only while visible inside the clip rect, loops modulo total duration (`oneshot = 0`) or stops on the final frame with no wake (`oneshot = 1`), yields to explicit `LayoutState.image_sources` overrides, and freezes on frame `0` with no wakeup under `MotionPreference::Reduced`.
- **Dynamic wrapper drawables (Wave 19A & Wave 19B):** Supports `RULE_DRAWABLE_DYNAMIC = 286` on both `src:` (`android:src` / `app:srcCompat`) and `bg:` (`android:background`, propagating through `<inset>`, `<ripple>`, and single-item `<layer-list>`) for `<level-list>` (`level|<min0>:<max0>:<key0>,...`), `<clip>` (`clip|<h|v>|<gravity>|<key>`), `<rotate>` (`rotate|<from_deg>,<to_deg>|<px>,<py>|<key>`), `<scale>` (`scale|<sw>,<sh>|<gravity>|<key>`), and `<transition>` (`transition|250|<key0>,<key1>`), preserving static fallback fields. Driven at runtime by `LayoutState::drawable_level: HashMap<String, u32>` (`0..=10000`) and `LayoutState::drawable_transition_active: HashMap<String, bool>` (internal `DrawableTransitionMotion`) with RTL-aware clipping, pivoted rotation/scaling (`PushTransform`/`PopTransform`), and timed `PushLayer`/`PopLayer` crossfades (instant snap under `MotionPreference::Reduced`).
- **`<animated-selector>` transition frame playback (Wave 20A & Wave 20B):** Supports `RULE_ANIMATED_SELECTOR = 293` (`"<ctx>:<from_mask>:<to_mask>:<rev:0|1>|<img_key_0>:<dur_ms_0>,..."`) across both `src`/`srcCompat` (`ctx="src"`) and `background` (`ctx="bg"`, propagating through `<inset>`/`<ripple>`/`<layer-list>`). `decode_animated_selector_transitions` in [`tools/import-android-layout.py`](../../../../tools/import-android-layout.py) parses `<item android:id>` 7-bit state masks and `<transition android:fromId android:toId android:reversible>` `<animation-list>` frames, bundling frames while preserving existing `96`/`231`/`253`/`254` state items. At runtime in [`codes/libs/ui/libbhoo/src/systems/android/xml/mod.rs`](../../src/systems/android/xml/mod.rs), a bounded `animated_selector_states` map (capped at `256` entries) tracks the previous 7-bit `live_state_mask`, plays forward/reverse frame sequences via `f.time()`/`f.wake_at(...)` for both `src` (RTL `autoMirrored`-aware) and `bg`, settles onto the destination stateful drawable on completion, and cuts directly to the destination with zero wakeups under `MotionPreference::Reduced`.
- **Sampled `<animated-vector>` keyframe playback (Wave 24A & Wave 24B):** Supports repeatable `RULE_ANIMATED_VECTOR = 309` (`"<ctx:src|bg>:<repeat:0|1>:<dur_ms>|<img_key_0>,...,<img_key_7>"`) across `android:src`/`app:srcCompat` and `android:background` (including `<inset>`/`<layer-list>`/`<ripple>` wrappers, external `@drawable/`/`@animator/` and inline `<aapt:attr>` definitions, `11` numeric group/path/alpha properties sampled into `8` evenly-spaced keyframes `<base>__avd0..7` + frame-0 static fallback via [`tools/android_vector.py`](../../../../tools/android_vector.py), propagating `autoMirrored` `255` and vector tint). At runtime in `codes/libs/ui/libbhoo/src/systems/android/xml/mod.rs`, `Kind::Icon` advances keyframes across `dur_ms` via `f.time()` and clip-gated `f.wake_at(...)`, looping (`repeat == 1`) or clamping on the final frame with `0` wakeups (`repeat == 0`), settling directly on `len - 1` (`repeat == 0`) or `0` (`repeat == 1`) under `f.reduced_motion()`, and honoring `LayoutState.image_sources`, tint, scale, alpha, rounded clipping, and RTL `autoMirrored`. Per-frame SVG `pathData` morphing between arbitrary topologies is not interpolated.
- **Borderless circular default state-layer ripple:** When `Kind::Icon` has `radius == 0.0`, no asymmetric `RULE_CORNER_RADII`, and `!self.has_explicit_custom_background()`, its state-layer/ripple radius defaults to `b.width.min(b.height) * 0.5` rather than a sharp `0dp` rectangle.

## Motion and animation

The button uses Bhoo's shared pointer state layer ([Feedback motion profile](../motion.md#motion-profiles)); `<animation-list>` drawables (`RULE_ANIMATION_LIST = 285`) step frames by `f.time()` with bounded `f.wake_at(...)` wakeups while visible and stop scheduling once a `oneshot` sequence completes; `<transition>` drawables (`RULE_DRAWABLE_DYNAMIC = 286`) crossfade via `PushLayer`/`PopLayer` over `dur_ms` (default `250ms`) when `LayoutState::drawable_transition_active` toggles and stop requesting frames once settled; `<animated-selector>` transitions (`RULE_ANIMATED_SELECTOR = 293`) play forward/reverse `<animation-list>` frame sequences via `f.time()`/`f.wake_at(...)` tracked in the bounded `animated_selector_states` map (capped at `256` entries) and settle onto the destination stateful drawable with no wakeups once finished; and `<animated-vector>` drawables (`RULE_ANIMATED_VECTOR = 309`) step across their `8` sampled keyframes over `dur_ms` via `f.time()` and clip-gated `f.wake_at(...)`, looping when `repeat == 1` or clamping on the final frame with `0` wakeups when `repeat == 0`.

**Reduced motion:** Activation and state feedback remain immediate without decorative motion, `<animation-list>` drawables freeze strictly on frame `0` without scheduling wakeups, `<transition>` drawables snap immediately to layer `0` or layer `1`, `<animated-selector>` transitions cut directly to the destination stateful drawable with zero wakeups, and `<animated-vector>` drawables settle immediately on `len - 1` (`repeat == 0`) or `0` (`repeat == 1`) with zero wakeups.

## Acceptance criteria

- Import a PNG or `<vector>` drawable and scale mode.
- Center small `24dp` icons inside `44dp`/`48dp` touch targets without upscaling when no explicit `scaleType`, padding, or `adjustViewBounds` is set, while scaling down oversized drawables cleanly.
- Activate the enabled image button and reject disabled activation.
- Compare appearance and motion with Android on a device.
