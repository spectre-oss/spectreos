## Scope and variants

Title, navigation affordance, actions; compact/expanded forms.

This is one family specification; variants share this document. The names describe proposed design scope, not new exported Rust APIs or completed upstream parity.

## State and interaction contract

Expose title, navigation and action slots with predictable focus order; define collapse behavior separately from content navigation.

The proposed public contract should expose the value/state and action results described above. The caller owns application data. Preserve stable IDs and prevent stale callbacks after removal. Final Rust signatures remain open until the supporting input, layout and semantic infrastructure is available.

## Motion and animation

Collapse in response to scroll position; blend elevation/title treatment without restarting on every wheel event.

Use the shared [layout profile](../motion.md#motion-profiles). Motion here is a Bhoo proposal, not a claim to reproduce official Material timing or physics. Functional state changes must not wait for visual completion. Reversals start from the current presented state; remove animation state when its owner disappears.

**Reduced motion:** Apply final geometry immediately and retain the same stable focus/selection identity; no sliding or size tween.

## Accessibility and RTL

Apply the semantic roles, names, focus and input requirements in the [shared component contract](README.md#shared-component-contract) where relevant. Decorative primitives must not create extra focus targets. Labels and editable text require correct shaping and bidirectional handling; interpret leading/trailing placement logically. Preserve the specific focus rules in this document when content becomes hidden or disabled.

## Performance and lifecycle

Apply the [motion scheduler and cache rules](../motion.md#scheduling-and-lifecycle). Avoid independent per-component timers. Static or settled instances must stop requesting frames; invisible indeterminate effects must pause. Restrict layout/geometry work to affected visible content and use bounded caches appropriate to this family's data.

## Acceptance criteria

- Scroll while focusing an action and verify collapsing does not hide or move focus unexpectedly.
- Verify normal and reduced-motion paths, interruption/reversal, disable/removal and final settled state where applicable.
- Verify pointer and applicable keyboard/semantic behavior independently of animation completion.
- Apply only relevant shared checks; document exceptions rather than inventing interactions for decorative primitives.

## Browser demonstration

[Open the live Top app bar preview](http://127.0.0.1:8766/#/top-app-bar).

Demonstrates: Small, center-aligned, medium and large app bars above wheel- and touch-scrollable content, with pinned, enter-always and exit-until-collapsed policies, navigation and action targets, and RTL placement. Back, search and more use bundled Google Material Icons; the back arrow mirrors in RTL. Sources and licenses are recorded in [third-party notices](../../../../../../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md). The content is keyboard-focusable and supports Up, Down, Home and End with a visible focus outline. The Rust API routes a delta between the caller-owned bar and content offsets and returns the consumed amount. Touch dragging uses a threshold and continues outside the viewport after capture. A fast release continues scrolling with bounded velocity decay; a partial bar then settles to the nearer endpoint. A new gesture interrupts motion, and reduced motion suppresses the fling and snaps immediately. The 64, 112 and 152 dp heights follow the local Apache-2.0 Compose `AppBar.kt` and app-bar token snapshots. Large titles crossfade to the small title as collapse proceeds.

Focused scrollable content also supports Page Up and Page Down, moving by the visible viewport height.

Remaining scope: General nested-scroll dispatch across arbitrary containers and platform accessibility bridging; exact Android fling decay, velocity tracking and spring-based settling; full Material Symbols, typography, color and title interpolation. The exponential decay and 250 ms settle curve approximate the Compose behavior and are not its spline and spring animations. The implementation is an initial approximation, not a claim of visual or motion parity.

See [demo coverage and motion baseline](../demo-coverage.md). The state/motion contracts above describe the target design; they are not all established by this preview.

- **Scroll-coupled `AppBarLayout` `layout_scrollFlags` & `layout_scrollEffect="compress"` (`RULE_APP_BAR_SCROLL_FLAGS = 284` + `RULE_APP_BAR_EXT = 290`, Wave 18A/18B + Wave 20A/20B):** Evaluates direct non-`CollapsingToolbarLayout` children of vertical `AppBarLayout` (`Kind::Linear`) carrying `scroll` against the sibling scroll offset (`state.collapsing_scroll_offset` / `state.offsets`) in `eval_app_bar_child_collapse` / `preview_app_bar_child_collapse`, shifting pinned following children (such as tabs) and `RULE_COORDINATOR_SCROLL_BEHAVIOR = 226` scrolling content upward cleanly:
  - `scroll`: collapses up to the child's full natural height, shifting its draw origin upward by `-collapse_px` and skipping draw once fully collapsed.
  - `exitUntilCollapsed`: caps maximum collapse at `(child_h + margin_range - min_h).max(0.0)` so the child remains pinned at `min_height`.
  - `enterAlways`: tracks per-frame scroll deltas (`LayoutState.app_bar_scroll_motion`) so scrolling upward reveals the collapsed bar immediately even far from `scroll = 0`, and scrolling downward collapses it again.
  - `enterAlwaysCollapsed`: when paired with `enterAlways` and `min_height > 0`, caps mid-scroll upward reveal at `min_height` until the scroll offset returns to the top.
  - `snap` (upgraded Wave 20A): when scroll pauses at an intermediate partial collapse, waits a `0.08s` scroll-idle debounce (`APP_BAR_SNAP_IDLE_DELAY_S`) and smoothly animates over `0.22s` (`APP_BAR_SNAP_SETTLE_DUR_S`) with quadratic ease-out to `0` (`< 50%`) or `max_collapse` (`>= 50%`), snapping immediately without intermediate frames under `MotionPreference::Reduced`.
  - `snapMargins` (Wave 20A): includes the child's vertical margins (`top + bottom`) in its collapse range and layout extents (`app_bar_child_collapsed_layout`) so top and bottom margins collapse cleanly alongside the bar body.
  - `layout_scrollEffect="compress"` (`RULE_APP_BAR_EXT = 290`, `"effect=compress"`, Wave 20A/20B): clips the scrolling child to its remaining visible band and applies a smooth quadratic compression translation as collapse progresses.
